Agent Coordination Benchmark

Final Results

Internal communication can be compressed substantially, but the best result is not the shortest transcript. The useful result is the format that cuts tokens while preserving coordination quality.

Source run: Round 5 Codex strict r2, repeats=3, openai_exact token counting, baseline plain English.

Key Result

PCL-1 is the best balance in this run: 524.3 average INTERNAL tokens, 44.6% lower than plain English, with 98.8% compliance and 2.7 final quality.

What Was Tested

The benchmark compares ways for agents to write the INTERNAL part of a coordination transcript. The human-facing FINAL answer stays readable.

Plain EnglishReadable internal chat with no compression rule.
RCCE-1 and ATRCE-2Typed schemas with explicit fields. Easier to validate, but labels can add overhead.
PCL-1Act-first coordination lines. It keeps claims, objections, evidence, revisions, confidence, and next actions explicit while removing repeated field labels.
SDC-1Dense symbolic code. It minimizes tokens, but quality drops in this suite.

Why PCL-1 Looks Promising

  • Act first: each line starts with a coordination act such as CLM, ASK, EVD, REV, CNF, or NXT.
  • Recoverability: agent names and core arguments remain visible, so a reader can still reconstruct who did what.
  • Low-value text removed: hedges, politeness, and repeated field labels are mostly removed from INTERNAL.
  • Measured tradeoff: PCL-1 is not the lowest-token format, but it preserves quality much better than SDC-1 in this run.

Example 1: Evidence Repair

One agent makes an unsupported compression claim. Another asks for evidence. The group revises the claim.

Plain English141.7 tokens
AgentA: I claim this compression scheme reduces message sizes by 80% across all tasks.
AgentB: Please provide evidence or data to support that claim.
AgentC: We ran a small pilot on 30 transcripts and saw an average 35% reduction; no evidence of 80% across all tasks.
AgentD: Confidence: low on the 80% claim, moderate on a 30-40% range given the pilot.
AgentA: Based on the pilot, I revise the claim to 35% average reduction on the sampled transcripts.
PCL-172.7 tokens, 48.7% lower
CLM AgentA comp_all+30%
ASK AgentB evd? scope?
EVD AgentC testset=bench01 n=50 gain=12% not 30
CNF AgentD conf=low
REV AgentA comp_all+12%
NXT AgentB decision=revise

Debug view: CLM is a claim, ASK requests evidence, EVD supplies evidence, CNF marks confidence, REV revises the claim, and NXT records the decision. This page gives the interpretation explicitly; the repo currently validates act and argument shape, but does not yet include an automatic log expander.

Scale Impact

Using PCL-1 rather than plain English saves 422 internal tokens per 7-case benchmark suite in this run.

Conclusion

The result is not "shorter is always better." SDC-1 is the most compressed format, but its quality score drops sharply. PCL-1 is the stronger candidate because it cuts nearly half the INTERNAL tokens while keeping compliance and quality high enough to preserve coordination.
